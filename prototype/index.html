What this prototype stands in for

  • Fake cells take the place of the merged mouse NASH, human cirrhosis and Liver Cell Atlas data (about 612,000 cells).
  • Disease score stands in for each cell's MELD likelihood of coming from a diseased liver.
  • Find similar runs a random walk with restart in the browser. The final tool sends the selection to a FastAPI /search endpoint.
  • Canvas drawing handles up to about 100,000 cells. The final map uses WebGL and loads cells on demand when zoomed in.
  • Signaling reads a precomputed table, as CellPhoneDB output per cell type and condition will be.
